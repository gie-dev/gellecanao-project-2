About the School

A Tradition of Faith and Learning

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. (KCC) is a Catholic educational institution located in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Its official seal bears the Chi-Rho symbol and the call of Matthew 28:19, “Teach ye all nations.”

Mission

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Vision

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to, and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

School History

Kabankalan Catholic College (KCC) began in 1927 as Kabankalan Institute, which was later renamed Kabankalan Private Academy. In 1953, the Columban Fathers took over the school and transferred it near the church. In 1963–1964, it expanded by offering college and elementary education. Over the years, KCC experienced changes in leadership, school development, and challenges, including the fire in 1987 that destroyed several buildings. In 1988, Kabankalan became a diocese. The school continued to grow through the support of religious congregations and church leaders. Today, KCC continues to provide quality Catholic education, promote Christian values, and develop responsible individuals who can contribute positively to society.
